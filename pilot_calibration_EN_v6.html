Pilot Study

Count the Zeros — EN v6 · Calibration

Welcome

This pilot study tests whether the new experimental design works as intended. You will play the role of either a Worker or a Manager for 12 rounds.

Please enter a name or pseudonym and choose your role.

How It Works

Task: Each round, you have 45 seconds to count the zeros in a 10×10 grid and submit your answer.

Performance Categories:

• Exactly correct (0 error) → £0.40 revenue for the manager, bonus up to £0.40
• Borderline (±1 error) → £0.20 revenue for the manager, bonus up to £0.20
• 2 or more errors → £0.00 revenue, no bonus

Earnings: In every round you are matched with a manager you receive a £0.10 base payment plus whatever bonus your manager decides to give you.

Manager: Sets a bonus for each round based on your performance. Different bonus ranges apply for exactly correct and borderline answers.

Leaving and the unemployment pool: At the end of each round you may choose to continue or leave. If you leave, you enter the unemployment pool and earn £0.10 per round. After waiting for some time you may be re-matched with a different manager — you cannot return to your previous manager.
