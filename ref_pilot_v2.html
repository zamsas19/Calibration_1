How it works

Each round, you will see a grid filled with zeros and ones. Your job is to count how many zeros there are, type your answer, and press Submit (or hit Enter).

You have a set amount of time for each round. Try to submit your answer before the timer runs out — but if it expires, that is fine, we just record it and move on.

After each round there is a short break screen before the next one begins.

There are no penalties for wrong answers. Just do your best and count as carefully as you can.
