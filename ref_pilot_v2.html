How it works

Each section, you will see a grid of numbers. Every cell is either a 0 or a 1. Your task: count how many zeros are in the grid, type your answer, and press Submit (or hit Enter).

As soon as you submit, a new grid appears immediately — keep going until the timer runs out. Try to do as many grids as you can within the time.

After each section there is a short summary screen before the next one begins.

There are no penalties for wrong answers. Just count as carefully as you can.
